June 1, 2009. An airliner vanishes over the Atlantic in the middle of the night. No mayday. No warning. For almost two years, the only answers lie nearly 4 kilometers below the surface, inside two small orange boxes.

This is the story of the black box: who invented it, how it's built to survive almost anything, how searchers find it on the bottom of the ocean, and what one recording finally revealed.

✈️ In this video:
• Why the black box isn't black, and why it's actually TWO boxes
• How investigators rebuild a flight second by second from the data
• David Warren: the boy who lost his father, and the scientist who invented the cockpit recorder
• The 6 torture tests: 3,400 G impacts, steel spikes, crushing, 1,100 °C fire, deep-sea pressure and toxic fluids
• Where the recorders are kept on the plane, and why
• The two-year search of the Atlantic sea floor, and the robots that finally found it
• What the recordings revealed: iced pitot tubes, an autopilot disconnect, and a high-altitude stall
• How the lessons changed pilot training and made flying safer
