# Why Planes Fly at 35,000 Feet: story bible

**Logline:** A curious passenger ("you") discovers why airliners cruise at 35,000 ft: a balance of thin air, lift, weather, wind and traffic, and a cruising height that keeps changing.
**Tone:** wonder, a clear documentary feel, a little suspense around the "coffin corner". Second person: "you" = PAX.

## Cast
| id | role | outfit | signature | expressions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAX | "you", the passenger | red-orange hoodie `#d64a33` | hoodie | curious, thinking, awe, smirk, scared |
| PILOT | the captain | dark suit + yellow cap `#f2c21a` | yellow cap | calm smile, focused, worried, determined |
| extras | other passengers | plain ink | none | neutral, sleepy |

## Locations
- **sky** (exterior, dusk/night/golden/storm): sea of clouds below the airliner, moon or low sun as the key, stars, haze bands, sometimes Everest poking through the clouds.
- **cabin** (interiorCool + dusk outside): oval windows glowing with the sunset (brightest area, PAX staged in front), overhead bins, rows of seat backs, dim aisle lights.
- **cockpit** (night/dawn): a wide windscreen with the dawn horizon, a dark glareshield, glowing instrument screens (key light on the pilot's face), overhead panel pinpoints.
- **sea** (dusk): PAX wading through water for the drag metaphor.
- **atmos** (storm): an atmosphere cross-section with weather in the bottom layer and a glowing tropopause line.
- **runway** (dusk): runway edge lights converging, a heavy airliner holding short.
- **blueprint**: all mechanisms and numbers (altitude scale, air density, lift, engine, speed squeeze, lanes, map, step climb).
- **3D**: a low-poly airliner over the clouds, a jet-stream globe, stacked flight lanes.

## Motifs
- An altitude tag in gold (`35,000 FT` / `FL350`) recurs and finally "cracks" in the twist.
- Air drawn as dots: dense = thick air, sparse = thin air.
- A red narrowing band = the coffin corner.

## Sound plan
No music (per the brief). Ambience: cabin hum indoors, wind outside. SFX on graphics (pop/blip), whooshes on fast moves, riser + impact on the coffin corner and the twist, freeze on the title.
